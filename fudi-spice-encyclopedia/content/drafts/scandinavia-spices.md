The clean, refined food of the Nordic countries carries some of the most distinctive spicing in Europe. The signature spices are cardamom, cinnamon, caraway, dill and nutmeg, with ginger, clove, allspice, liquorice, mustard, saffron and juniper in support.

## The Viking cardamom

Cardamom is the most notable of them all. It was supposedly brought north by the Vikings from Constantinople 1,000 years ago, and it is still a Nordic favourite. Danish pastries and other Nordic bakes rely on the festive flavours of cinnamon, clove, ginger and allspice.

## Pickling, preserving and juniper

The region's love of pickling and preserving produces a profusion of dill seed and native juniper. Juniper also appears with game, and in Finland's sahti beer.

## Two unusual treats

Salty liquorice is a genuinely unique Scandinavian treat. And bitter orange powder, made from dried Seville orange peel, is common in Finland, where it goes into biscuits, cakes and the traditional Easter dessert mämmi.

## Finnish gingerbread spice

Mix 1 tablespoon of bitter orange powder, 2 teaspoons of ground cloves, 2 teaspoons of ground ginger, 2 teaspoons of ground cinnamon and 1 teaspoon of ground cardamom. It is enough to flavour the dough for around 30 of the classic Finnish gingerbread biscuits, piparkakut.
