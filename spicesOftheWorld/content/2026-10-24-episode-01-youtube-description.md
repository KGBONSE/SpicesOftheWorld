## Description

I grew up in Ghana, and my favourite memories are trips to Makola Market in Accra with my mum, God bless her soul. She always stopped at one stall first, cracked a small reddish-brown seed between her fingers and let me smell it. That seed is grains of paradise, the spice Europe once borrowed from West Africa and then forgot.

In this first episode of Spices of the World we go from Makola Market to a farm in Sidcup, London: where grains of paradise comes from, how it crossed the Sahara to Europe, why it faded, what makes it taste the way it does, and how it ends up in Yaji, the dry rub behind Nigerian suya and Ghanaian chinchinga.

Yaji is made from grains of paradise, grains of selim, dried chillies, crushed kuli-kuli (peanut puffs), ginger, a crumbled stock cube, salt and black pepper. The list is a starting point, not a complete recipe: add your own spices and change the proportions to suit your taste. Contains peanuts.

Full recipe and the ready-made blend: https://fudipeople.com
