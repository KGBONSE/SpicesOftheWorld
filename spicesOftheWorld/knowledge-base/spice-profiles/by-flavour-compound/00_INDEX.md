## 🌍 Africa-Relevant Spices (Priority for Fudi People Content)
1. **Grains of Paradise** ⭐ — West African origin, Ghana is the main modern producer, Yoruba spiritual use, "Grain Coast" history
2. **Grains of Selim** — Pan-African (Ethiopia to Ghana), used in Senegalese coffee, Nigerian peppersoup and jollof rice
3. **Tamarind** — Native to East Africa despite its Arabic/Indian-sounding name
4. **Sesame** — Originated in sub-Saharan Africa
5. **Piri Piri** (chilli variety, see chilli.md) — African/Mozambican-Portuguese use

## Notes on Content
This knowledge base paraphrases and reorganizes factual/historical information from the source material in original wording — it is not a verbatim reproduction of the book's text, food-partner lists, or recipes. Each file includes: taste profile, botanical name, origin & history, flavour chemistry, pairing logic, and culinary technique notes. Use this as reference material for script writing, the Knowledge & Brand Voice agent, and episode research — not as a substitute for the original published book.
