# Sweet and Spicy Apple Pastry Rosettes 🌍

This beguiling take on a popular pastry treat is inspired by the sweet and peppery flavours of East and West African spiced teas. The sweetness of the apples balances the lingering heat supplied by Scotch bonnet chillies; if unavailable, use African or Thai bird's eye chillies instead.

**Makes 6 pastries | Prep 30 mins | Cook 35–40 mins**

## Ingredients
- 2 red apples (Empire, Jazz, or Pink Lady), juice of ½ lemon
- 3 tbsp apricot jam
- ½ Scotch bonnet chilli, deseeded and thinly sliced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon, ¼ tsp ground cloves
- ¼ tsp ground grains of paradise
- ¼ tsp ground cardamom seeds
- 375g (13oz) ready-rolled puff pastry sheet
- Butter, for greasing

## Method Summary
1. Slice apples into thin half-moons; simmer briefly in lemon juice and water (or microwave) until just pliable, then cool.
2. Melt apricot jam with chilli and spices.
3. Roll pastry into a rectangle and slice into six strips.
4. Spread spiced jam over each strip, arrange apple slices overlapping along the length.
5. Fold and roll each strip into a rose shape, keeping apple slices in place.
6. Place in a greased cupcake tin and bake at 210°C (410°F/Gas 6½) for 35–40 minutes until golden.

## Spice Ideas from the Book
- Substitute a different pungent spice for the chilli heat: black pepper for floral woodiness, or Sichuan pepper for tingling citrus heat.
- Experiment with other sweet, warming spices inspired by spiced tea flavours, such as allspice and nutmeg.
- Add dried chilli flakes to the apricot jam for toasted, smoky notes instead of fresh chilli.
