# West African Peanut Curry with Durban Masala 🌍

Inspired by the peanut soups and stews of West Africa, this recipe forgoes the usual fiery Scotch bonnet chillies for a twist from South Africa's Eastern Cape — a Durban curry masala spice blend. Heat comes mainly from cayenne pepper; use less for a milder kick. Add more water at the end for a hearty soup instead.

**Serves 6–8 | Prep 20 mins | Cook 25–35 mins**

## For the Durban Masala Blend
- 2 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp coriander seeds
- ½ tsp fenugreek seeds
- Seeds of 5 cardamom pods
- 1 cinnamon stick, 5 cloves
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- ½ tsp ground ginger

## For the Curry
- 4 tbsp unsweetened peanut butter
- 500ml (16fl oz) water, 2 tbsp oil
- 3 large carrots, 2 parsnips, peeled
- 1 swede or turnip (~500g)
- 4 baby aubergines or white garden egg aubergines (optional)
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 400g (14oz) mushrooms
- 1 tbsp tomato purée, 2 ripe tomatoes, diced
- 500ml (16fl oz) vegetable stock
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method Summary
1. Grind whole spices for the Durban masala and combine with cayenne and ginger.
2. Blend peanut butter with water until smooth; simmer ~15 minutes until oil rises to the surface.
3. Fry carrots and parsnips in oil until browned; set aside.
4. Sauté onion with spices, add tomato purée and chopped tomatoes, then mushrooms; fry briefly.
5. Add stock, peanut sauce, and the vegetables (including chopped aubergine if using); simmer 15–20 minutes until tender.
6. Season, remove whole spices, and serve with sticky rice — or thin with more water for a soup.

## Spice Ideas from the Book
- Blend your own masala: swap in black cardamom or grains of Selim for smokiness, or replace ginger/coriander with woody, balsamic mace.
- Dry fry whole spices to create new toasted pyrazine flavours (use caution — they can dominate).
- Add fresh spices to the frying vegetables for a deeper base flavour.
