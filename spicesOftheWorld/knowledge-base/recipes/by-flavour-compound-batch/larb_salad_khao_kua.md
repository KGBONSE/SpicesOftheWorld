# Asian Larb Salad with Curried Duck and Khao Kua

Popular in both Laos and Thailand, larb is a fiery salad most often made with finely minced meat or fish, flavoured with fresh spices and herbs and topped with roasted rice powder (khao kua). This version mixes in garam masala — the warm, aromatic mix at the heart of much Indian cooking.

**Serves 2–3 | Prep 10 mins | Cook 2–3½ mins**

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp Thai chilli flakes (or other crushed dried chillies)
- 2 duck fillets, skin removed, minced
- Juice of 2 limes, 2 tbsp Thai fish sauce (nam pla)
- 1 tbsp palm sugar or muscovado sugar
- 4 shallots, finely sliced
- 2 lemongrass stalks
- Handful each fresh mint and coriander leaves, chopped
- 1 tbsp khao kua roasted rice powder

## Method Summary
1. Heat oil, add garam masala and chilli flakes, stir-fry until fragrant.
2. Add duck mince, fry until browned outside but still pink inside; set aside.
3. Mix lime juice and fish sauce with sugar for the dressing.
4. Trim, peel, and finely chop lemongrass stalks after crushing to release oils.
5. Combine warm duck, shallots, lemongrass, mint, and coriander into the dressing.
6. Sprinkle with khao kua just before serving to keep it crunchy.

## Spice Ideas from the Book
- Reinvent the citrus sour in khao kua by replacing/enhancing lemongrass and lime leaves with lemon myrtle, sumac, or dried lime powder.
- Use tamarind water instead of lime juice for a fruitier sweet-sour dressing.
- Personalize the garam masala with coriander and green cardamom for floral character, or fennel/dill/caraway for aniseed undertones.
