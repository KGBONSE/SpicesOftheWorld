# Date and Tamarind Granita with Caramelized Pineapple

In this innovative ice, warming notes of cumin and garam masala contrast with astringent tamarind, zesty ginger, and the herbal pungency of fresh green chillies — a combination inspired by South Asian street food, where these spices work together as thirst quenchers in intense summer heat.

**Serves 6 as a starter | Prep 2 hrs | Cook 30–35 mins**

## For the Granita
- ¾ tsp cumin seeds
- 125g (4½oz) jaggery or palm sugar, chopped
- 50g (1¾oz) liquid glucose, 750ml (1¼ pints) water
- 75g (2½oz) fresh root ginger, chopped
- 1 large lime (rind only, no pith)
- 150g (5½oz) seedless tamarind block
- 100g (3½oz) pitted dates, chopped
- 2–3 green chillies, chopped with seeds
- Large bunch fresh mint
- ½ tsp garam masala, ½ tsp black salt

## For the Caramelized Pineapple
- 4 tbsp icing sugar, 2 tsp chaat masala
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder or hot paprika
- 300g (10oz) fresh pineapple, diced
- Handful fresh mint leaves

## Method Summary
1. Toast and grind cumin seeds.
2. Dissolve jaggery/palm sugar with glucose and water; simmer 3–4 minutes.
3. Add lime rind, ginger, tamarind, dates, and chillies; simmer 20 minutes until softened.
4. Stir in mint, infuse 30 minutes, then sieve the pulp into a bowl.
5. Whisk in garam masala, black salt, and ground cumin; adjust sweetness.
6. Freeze in a shallow container, stirring occasionally, until set (1+ hour) — or churn in an ice-cream maker.
7. Coat diced pineapple in a chaat masala/chilli/icing sugar mix, then caramelize in a hot dry pan for 5–8 minutes.
8. Rake the frozen granita with a fork and serve topped with caramelized pineapple and mint.

## Spice Ideas from the Book
- Replace amchoor in the chaat masala with ground anardana for a more bitter tang.
- Add whole star anise and toasted fennel seeds to the date syrup for liquorice-lovers.
- Strengthen the ginger by using ground ginger instead of fresh, for a nuttier warmth.
