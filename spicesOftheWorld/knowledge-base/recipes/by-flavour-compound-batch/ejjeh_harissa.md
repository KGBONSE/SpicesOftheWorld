# Ejjeh with Courgette, Feta, and Dill and Black Lime Harissa

*Ejjeh* is a Lebanese omelette of fresh herbs and courgettes, normally spiced with a regional baharat blend, but enlivened here with North African harissa. This twist swaps dill seeds for caraway in the harissa, black lime for preserved lemon, and uses chipotle instead of smoked paprika for smokiness.

**Serves 2–3 | Prep 10 mins | Cook 30 mins**

## For the Harissa
- 5 large red chillies, 1 red pepper
- 1 tbsp chipotle chilli paste
- ½ tsp dried chilli flakes
- ½ tsp cumin seeds, toasted and ground
- ½ tsp dill seeds, toasted and ground
- 2 large garlic cloves, crushed
- 1½ tsp red wine vinegar
- 3 tbsp olive oil, pinch of sea salt
- ½ tsp black lime powder

## For the Omelette
- 1 tbsp olive oil, plus extra
- 1 courgette, grated
- Large handful each parsley and mint, chopped
- 1 tbsp harissa (more for heat)
- 200g (7oz) feta, drained and chopped
- 4 large eggs, beaten and seasoned
- Sea salt and black pepper

## Method Summary
1. Roast chillies and pepper until charred; peel, deseed, and chop.
2. Blend with remaining harissa ingredients until smooth. Makes enough for ~15 tbsp; keeps a week refrigerated.
3. Cook grated courgette in olive oil until golden.
4. Sprinkle over herbs, dot with harissa, add feta and black pepper. Pour in eggs, cook until the base is firm but top still liquid, then finish under the grill until risen but slightly gooey.
5. Serve stuffed into pitta with a za'atar-dressed radish and green leaf salad.

## Spice Ideas from the Book
- Swap dill for other aniseed-spectrum spices: anise, star anise, allspice, or liquorice.
- Experiment with sweet-sour substitutes for black lime: sumac, barberry, amchoor, anardana, tamarind, lemon myrtle.
