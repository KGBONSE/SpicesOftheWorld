## Spiced Scallops with Saffron Beurre Blanc
*Serves 6 as a starter · Prep 30 min + 1hr soaking · Cook 2hr25 + cooling*
**Pairs with:** Saffron profile.

Grind saffron strands, soak in warm water 1 hour. Make a spiced mushroom seasoning: bake thinly sliced chestnut mushrooms low and slow until crisp, then process with toasted ground fennel seeds, ground ginger, and salt. For the beurre blanc: reduce white wine vinegar and white wine with diced shallot and peppercorns to a syrup; strain, whisk in the saffron and its soaking water, then emulsify with cubed butter over low heat; sharpen with lemon. Coat scallops in olive oil and the spiced mushroom mix, sear 1–2 min per side. Plate over beurre blanc, scatter with chives.

**Spice ideas for variation:** swap fennel/ginger for curry leaves, mustard seeds, and cumin for a South Indian-style seasoning; pair fennel with cumin and smoked hot paprika for a Mediterranean palette; lightly crack the peppercorns for stronger pepper notes in the beurre blanc.

## Spiced Filipino Adobo with Chicken and Pork
*Serves 4–6 · Prep 25 min · Cook 1 hour*

Grind cardamom seeds, star anise, cinnamon, chilli flakes, and peppercorns in a pestle and mortar; add garlic and ginger, pound to a rough paste. Fry the paste in oil 2–3 minutes until fragrant. Stir in chopped spring onions and palm sugar, then add chicken thighs/drumsticks and pork tenderloin chunks, coating in the spice mix. Pour in vinegar, soy sauce, and chicken stock; add bay leaves; bring to the boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer gently 1 hour until tender. Serve with steamed rice and stir-fried greens.

**Spice ideas for variation:** replace the first three sweetly fragranced spices with a more earthy mix of cumin, paprika, and turmeric; use sulphurous spices (mustard, curry leaves, asafoetida) to emphasize the meatiness; replace vinegar with tamarind water and add lemongrass to the paste for an altered citrus-sour profile.

## Durban Beef Bunny Chow
*Serves 4 · Prep 30 min · Cook 40–50 min*

Fry cardamom, cinnamon, and fennel seeds in oil until aromatic. Add chopped onion, soften 5–8 min. Stir in Durban curry masala, ginger, garlic, and tomato purée, cook 1 min. Add chopped tomatoes, cook 4–5 min until sauce-like. Stir in stewing beef, potato, curry leaves, and water; season; simmer covered 40–50 min until meat is tender. Stir in coriander and a squeeze of lime. Discard cinnamon/cardamom/curry leaves. Divide between hollowed-out halves of small crusty white bread loaves; garnish with extra coriander.

## Chana Masala with Sweet Potato and Spinach
*Serves 4 · Prep 20 min · Cook 30–35 min*

Fry cumin seeds in oil until aromatic; add onion, soften 5–8 min. Add garlic and ginger, stir 1 min; add ground coriander, paprika, and turmeric, stir 2 min. Add sweet potato, coat well. Stir in tomatoes and chickpeas (with liquid, or reserved cooking water if using dried), plus chilli pieces; simmer covered 25–30 min until potato is tender and sauce has thickened. Add spinach, stir 3–4 min until wilted. Stir in garam masala, adjust seasoning with lemon juice. Serve with naan.

## Masor Tenga Fish Curry
*Serves 4 · Prep 10 min + 10–15 min marinating · Cook 30 min*

Rub sea bream/grey mullet fillets with salt and turmeric; set aside 10–15 min. Fry mustard seeds in oil; add fish skin-side down, cook until crisp and just cooked through; set aside. Add remaining oil, heat high, add panch phoran, let seeds splutter and pop 1 min. Turn heat to medium, add onion and chillies, fry gently 3–4 min until softened. Add chopped tomatoes, cook 4–5 min until they soften. Add mustard paste and chilli flakes, cook 4–5 min. Pour in water and lime juice, bring to a gentle simmer. Add fried fish back in, skin-side up, heat through 2–3 min. Garnish with coriander, serve with rice.

## Goan Vindaloo
*Serves 4 · Prep 30 min + 1hr–overnight marinating · Cook 30–40 min*
**Pairs with:** Vindaloo Paste blend (South Asia file).

Marinate chicken/pork and aubergine chunks in vindaloo paste with garlic and ginger, at least 1 hour or overnight. Fry onion in oil until soft and golden (10–15 min). Add the meat, aubergine, and marinade, brown 4–5 minutes. Add tomatoes, chillies, and stock, bring to a simmer; stir in jaggery and vinegar until dissolved. Cover, transfer to oven (190°C/375°F/Gas 5), cook 30–40 min until tender and sauce has thickened. Sprinkle with coriander, serve with rice, yoghurt, and lime pickle.

## Prawn Summer Rolls
*Makes 12 · Prep 45 min*

Soak dried rice noodles in hot water 3 min, drain and rinse. Dip a rice paper wrapper in hot water 10–15 seconds until pliable, pat dry. Lay 3 Thai basil leaves face down near the bottom edge, top with 4 prawn halves in a row. Add lettuce, noodles, carrot, cucumber, spring onion, coriander, mint, and peanuts. Squeeze lime juice over the filling. Fold the near edge over the filling, tuck under, and roll tightly, folding in the ends halfway through. Repeat with remaining wrappers. Serve at room temperature with *nuoc cham* dipping sauce.

## Nanjing Salted Duck
*Serves 4 · Prep 20 min + overnight marinating · Cook 15 min + 2hr cooling*
**Pairs with:** Nanjing Spice Bag blend (East Asia file).

Dry-roast salt and crushed Sichuan peppercorns in a pan 5–8 min until yellow/brown; cool. Score duck skin, rub the salt/pepper mix into skin and flesh; cover loosely, marinate overnight in the fridge. Lay duck pieces in a large pan with the salt/pepper, cover with water, add a spice bag, ginger, and spring onion; bring to the boil, then simmer gently 15 min. Turn off heat, cool duck in the covered liquid. Transfer to a board; discard or reserve cooking liquid as stock. Drizzle sesame oil over each piece; slice breast, divide legs at the joint. Serve warm or cool.

## Prawn and Vegetable Stir-Fry
*Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 10 min*
**Pairs with:** Five-Spice Powder blend (East Asia file).

Soak egg noodles in boiling water, covered, while preparing the stir-fry. Stir-fry onion and pepper in oil over high heat 2 min until softened/charred. Add ginger, garlic, and prawns, stir constantly 2 min until prawns are just pink. Add baby corn with soy sauce and rice wine, simmer 1 min. Stir in mangetout and five-spice powder, remove from heat. Drain noodles, stir in sesame seeds and sesame oil. Serve immediately with the hot stir-fry.

## Piparkakut (Finnish Gingerbread Biscuits)
*Makes about 40 · Prep 20 min + cooling, chilling, resting · Cook 10–12 min*
**Pairs with:** Finnish Gingerbread Spice blend (Europe file).

Melt honey, black treacle, butter, and sugar together over low heat; cool at least 10 min. Sift flour, bicarbonate of soda, gingerbread spice mix, and white pepper into a bowl. Pour in the treacle mixture, crack in an egg, stir to a smooth dark paste. Cover, chill at least 1 hour (up to overnight). Bring to room temperature, knead into a ball. Preheat oven to 190°C/375°F/Gas 5. Roll dough 2–3mm thick, cut into shapes, space on a lined tray (press in glacé ginger pieces if decorating). Bake 10–12 min. Cool on tray 10 min, then transfer to a rack. Stores airtight up to 1 week.

## Paella
*Serves 6 · Prep 30 min · Cook 50 min*
**Pairs with:** Paella Mix blend (Europe file) and Saffron profile.

Brown chicken, pancetta, and chorizo in olive oil, 8–10 min; remove and set aside. Cook onion and peppers in the remaining oil 5–8 min until softened. Stir in paella spice mix and rice, coat grains until glossy. Add sherry, let bubble and absorb; pour in hot stock and tomato passata, simmer 10 min, stirring occasionally. Return meat to the pan, cook a further 10 min. Stir in squid rings with lemon juice and zest, season. Push clams and mussels into the rice, cover, steam 4–5 min until shells open (discard any that don't). Sit crevettes on top, cover, steam a further 5 min until rice is tender and most liquid absorbed — don't stir, to let a crust form. Scatter with parsley, serve with lemon wedges.
