The Statue of Liberty

A gift from France for America’s 100th birthday — sculpted by one Frenchman, engineered by the man behind the Eiffel Tower.

1886
Dedicated October 28, with President Grover Cleveland presiding
305 ft
Height from ground to torch tip — 93 metres
2.4 mm
Thickness of the copper skin — about two pennies stacked
Gustave Eiffel
Engineered the internal iron frame, years before his tower
