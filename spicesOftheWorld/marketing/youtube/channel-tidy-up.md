# YouTube channel tidy-up (@fudi.people)

Do this in YouTube Studio on a computer (studio.youtube.com). About 30 minutes in total; tick them off in any order.
Source of truth for the plan: `video/episode-01/HANDOFF.md` and `marketing/social/2026-09-29-youtube-and-facebook-setup.md`.

## How to retitle one video
Studio -> Content -> click the video's thumbnail -> Details -> Title -> type the new title -> Save.

## How to unlist one video
Studio -> Content -> hover the video -> click the Visibility column ("Public") -> Unlisted -> Save.
(Unlisted videos stay on the channel for anyone with the link but disappear from search, the channel page and suggestions.)
