## How one post goes out to five platforms

| Content | Instagram | Facebook | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Photo post** (Tue) | Feed post | Same post | – | Community tab (optional) | Photo + short text |
| **Video** (Fri) | Reel | Reel | Video | Short | Video + short text |

Post once on Instagram and tick "Share to Facebook". Upload the same video
file to TikTok and YouTube Shorts. X gets the shorter text below.
Early evening (6 to 8pm) is a good default time. Being consistent matters more
than the exact time.
