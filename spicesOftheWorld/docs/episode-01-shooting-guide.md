# Episode 1 Shooting Guide — West Africa: Yaji

A practical, no-experience-needed walkthrough for filming
`scripts/episode-01-west-africa-yaji.md` yourself. Also published as a
formatted, printable page (ask Claude for the current artifact link).

## 0. Before You Touch Record

- **Lock the Yaji recipe.** Mix a small test batch using the v1 ratio card
  in the script, taste it, adjust, and write down your final ratios —
  before filming the Blend or Dish sections, or you'll reshoot later.
- **Phone, held vertically.** Portrait orientation, matches everything
  else in the library. No special settings needed.
- **Something to rest the phone on.** A stack of books, a shelf, anything
  steady. A cheap phone tripod helps but isn't required to start.
- **A quiet-ish room** for the talking-to-camera sections.

## 1. Two Golden Rules

**Sound matters more than picture.** Viewers forgive shaky video; they
don't forgive audio they can't hear over background noise. Film quiet,
phone within arm's reach — 1 to 1.5m from your face, closer than feels
natural.

**Light your face, don't backlight it.** Face a window — don't put it
behind you, or you'll be a silhouette. Overcast daylight through glass
beats any lamp.

## 2. Delivering the Lines

You don't need to memorise anything word-perfect.

- **Prop the script near the lens.** Printed page or a tablet just under
  the camera, so your eyes stay close to it while reading.
- **3–4 short takes, not one perfect one.** Say the line, pause, say it
  again slightly differently. Pick the best later — or send me all of them.
- **Talk like you're telling a friend.** Pause where you'd naturally
  pause. Fumble a sentence? Pause two seconds and redo just that sentence.

## 3. Shooting Order — By Location, Not Script Order

Don't shoot in the order the script reads — group everything by location.
Biggest time-saver there is.

### Session 1 — Talking to Camera (indoors, quiet room, window light)
Same spot, same outfit, filmed back to back so they cut together:
1. **Intro** (~10 sec) — "Today we're going back to West Africa…"
2. **Personal Hook** — the Mokola Market memory. The emotional heart of
   the episode — take your time, a few takes.
3. **Geography & Origin** — "Grains of paradise… West Africa… Ghana…"
4. **Trade & Migration History** — "Arab and Berber traders…"
5. **The Science** — "Paradol… ginger's heat compound…"

### Session 2 — Kitchen (once the recipe is locked)
6. **The Blend** — say the Yaji ingredient list while mixing the rub.
   Camera close on hands/bowl — or record talking and mixing as separate
   clean takes if doing both at once is too much to juggle.
7. **Close-up B-roll** — raw spices, grinding, the finished rub's texture.

### Session 3 — Outdoors / Grill
8. **The Dish** — skewers soaking, seasoning, grilling, the water/beer
   spray technique, rubbing Yaji on partway through, finished skewers.
9. **Close-ups of the crust forming** — these sell the dish more than
   wide shots.
10. **"Yaji doesn't stop at skewers…" narration** — can be voiced
    separately over this footage, doesn't need to be said while grilling.
