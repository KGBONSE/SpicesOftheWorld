# Fudi People — HACCP Summary (Reference)

⚠️ Reference only. Not a substitute for sign-off from a qualified food
safety professional / your local Environmental Health Officer before
changing any process.

## Process basis

Production uses a pressure canner/autoclave operating at **116–121°C**,
out of a home kitchen registered with the local Environmental Health
Officer.

## Key finding

Dried garlic, used across two flavour lines, was flagged as a
**higher-risk ingredient** in the hazard analysis.

## Priority recommended action

Validate the heat-process step (the pressure canning parameters) to confirm
it reliably addresses the garlic-related hazard, rather than relying on
general process assumptions.

## Status

Analysis complete; validation of the heat process is the outstanding
action item. Revisit this doc once validation testing is scheduled or
completed.
