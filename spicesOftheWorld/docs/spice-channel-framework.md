# [Your Channel Name] — Spice Journey Framework

## Core Story (Your Through-Line)
Every episode should loop back to this: a kid at Mokola-style markets in Ghana → a chili farm and okra patch in London → a real spice/chili oil brand. You're not a food historian doing a report — you're a farmer/founder tracing where your ingredients come from.

---

## Episode Template (repeat per region)

**1. Cold open — the personal hook (30–60 sec)**
A moment from your life connected to this region's spice: a market memory, a farm task, a family dish.

**2. Geography & origin (2–3 min)**
Where the spice/blend actually comes from — climate, soil, trade routes that carried it.

**3. History (2–3 min)**
How it moved: trade, colonization, migration, diaspora. Why it became central to that region's food.

**4. The blend (2–3 min)**
Break down the actual spice blend used in that region/country — ratios, what each spice contributes.

**5. The dish (5–8 min)**
Cook a real regional dish using that blend, tying back to your farm-grown or brand spices where possible.

**6. Close — the throughline**
Connect back to your journey/brand without a hard sell. "This is why I grow my own chilies."
