### 2 · Geography & Origin
South Africa, Mozambique, Botswana, Zimbabwe — Southern Africa. South
Africa calls itself the "Rainbow Nation," and nowhere is that truer than in
the food. Dutch colonisation brought Indonesian-influenced spicing into
Cape Malay cooking; Indian indentured labourers under British rule turned
Durban into a genuine global contender for curry.

*On-screen: "Durban — a global contender for curry" · B-roll: map graphic*

### 3 · Trade & Migration History
This region has some of the most direct, traceable colonial spice-trade
history we've covered — Dutch, British, Portuguese, and Indonesian/Indian
influence, all layered into one cuisine. Mozambique carries
Portuguese-influenced piri piri; Botswana and Zimbabwe use spice far more
sparingly than their neighbours. But Durban is the real story here — its
curry tradition isn't inspired by India, it's literally Indian cooking,
transplanted through indentured labour and never left.

*On-screen: "Durban curry — literally Indian cooking, transplanted" ·
B-roll: historical trade route map animation*

### 4 · The Science
Coriander is the spice tying this one together, and it's ancient — an
8,000-year-old stockpile was found in a cave in Israel. Its flavour comes
mostly from linalool, that lilac-floral note. And here's a nice callback —
coriander bridges with cumin, the spice from our Maghreb episode, through a
shared compound called cymene. Different regions, same chemistry.

Coriander's picking up real research interest too — early studies point to
genuine blood sugar and cholesterol benefits, alongside cumin's own
digestive and metabolic backing from a couple of episodes ago. And this is
a curry masala, so it's stacked: fenugreek is one of the best-supported
spices going for blood sugar, cinnamon has its own trial evidence there,
cardamom brings a heart angle, and ginger and chilli round it out. Curry
gets a bad rap — the spice mix underneath it is one of the healthiest
things in your cupboard.

*On-screen: "Coriander + cumin share real chemistry — cymene" / "Fenugreek
+ cinnamon — both researched for blood sugar" / "The spice mix under a
curry is one of the healthiest things in your cupboard" · B-roll: close-up
of coriander seeds, toasting*

### 5 · The Blend — Durban Curry Masala
Toast and grind coriander seeds, cumin seeds, cardamom seeds, fenugreek
seeds, cloves, a cinnamon stick, mild chilli powder, and cayenne pepper,
with ground ginger stirred through.

*On-screen: "DURBAN CURRY MASALA: coriander + cumin + cardamom + fenugreek

## Session 3 — Kitchen / Stove

### 6 · The Dish — Durban Curry
*(Can be voiced over the footage.)*

That same masala is what's inside Durban's most famous export — bunny chow,
curry ladled into a hollowed-out loaf of bread — and it turns up in
Durban-style samoosas too. Today we're making it the classic way, in a
bowl.

Durban curry, built entirely on that masala — the same blend, different
protein depending on what's local. Simple to make the point: this tastes
like India because it is India, just grown into something South African
too.

*On-screen: "Same masala also builds: bunny chow · Durban samoosas" then
"DURBAN CURRY" · B-roll: curry simmering, finished plate*
