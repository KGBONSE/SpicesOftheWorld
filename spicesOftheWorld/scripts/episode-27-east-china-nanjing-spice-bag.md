## 2. Geography & Origin

Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Shanghai sit on the coast, building a refined,
almost gourmet cuisine around abundant seafood and river food. Inland,
Anhui's Yellow Mountains and Huangshan area are the source of unique
foraged ingredients, often sweetened with sugar into hearty peasant food —
a genuinely different cuisine sharing a border with the coast's more
delicate cooking.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]:** "Coastal refinement, inland foraging — one region, two cuisines"

## 3. Trade & Migration History

This region's food owes its character to terrain more than trade — the
Yellow Mountains shaping Anhui's foraged, peasant tradition on one side,
the coastline shaping Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Shanghai's refined seafood
cooking on the other. One ingredient worth pulling out here: lotus seed, a
mild, sweet flavour reminiscent of fresh almonds and pine nuts, showing up
in dishes across this coast in a way it doesn't further north.

## 4. The Science

Ginger, sliced fresh into the Nanjing spice bag's cooking liquid, is one
of the most robustly evidenced spices in this entire knowledge base.
Multiple clinical trials confirm it reduces nausea and vomiting — the NHS
itself recommends it — and separate research has shown ginger
supplementation meaningfully reducing fasting blood sugar and HbA1c in
people with type 2 diabetes over 8 to 13 weeks.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT]:** "Ginger for nausea: backed by multiple clinical trials, recommended by the NHS"

## 5. The Blend — Nanjing Spice Bag

Combine 6 cloves, 4 star anise, 1 tsp crushed whole white pepper, 6 bay
leaves, dried tangerine peel, 1 tsp liquorice root powder, and 1 tsp salt
in a muslin bag; tie tightly and drop into the cooking water alongside
sliced fresh ginger and Shaoxing wine.

## 6. The Dish

This spice bag has one famous job: flavouring the stock for Nanjing salted
duck, a dish this city is known for worldwide. Elsewhere on this coast,
Shanghai's soup dumplings and Anhui's sweetened, foraged peasant dishes
tell a completely different story from the same stretch of country — but
today we're staying with what the spice bag itself was built for.
