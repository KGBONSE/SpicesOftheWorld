## Direct-to-consumer email

Design sequences with: purpose, audience segment, trigger, number of emails, send timing, exit condition, and full copy for every email (subject line + preview text + body + CTA). Offer 2 subject-line variants for A/B testing.

Standard sequences to offer: **welcome** (who Kofi is, Mokola Market, the farm, first blend suggestion), **new blend / new region launch**, **post-purchase** (how to use the blend, the dish from the episode, invite to the YouTube channel), **win-back**.

Voice: the reflective register, written as if Kofi is writing personally. Subject lines and CTA buttons may be short; the body is not ad copy. Each email carries one story (a farm moment, a market memory, one spice fact from the knowledge base with attribution) and one action.

Every email needs: unsubscribe line, company name (Fudi People Ltd), and only consented recipients. Do not state allergen information unless confirmed; link to the product page instead.

## Wholesale outreach

Targets: independent delis, grocers, African/Asian/Caribbean food shops, restaurants, farm shops, gift boxes. Draft:

1. **A buyer one-pager outline**: story, range (dry blends and chilli oils that are actually live), the three lines (Africa, South Asia, East Asia), farmer-founder angle, ordering route.
2. **First-touch email** (under 120 words), one follow-up at 5-7 days, one final at 14 days. Plain, warm, specific to the type of shop.
3. **Prospect list template** (business, type, area, contact route, status, notes). Use only publicly listed business contact details. Never invent contacts.

Trade pricing, minimum order, lead times, case sizes, shelf life and label compliance are not in the repo: mark each `[OPEN: ...]`. Do not invent them. Note that a registered home kitchen may limit who will stock the products; flag `[OPEN: buyer requirements on premises/certification]` for Kofi to confirm rather than guessing.

Save to `marketing/email/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` or `marketing/wholesale/...`. End with a "Needs Kofi" list.
