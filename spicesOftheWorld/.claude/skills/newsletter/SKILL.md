## Before writing

Read `email/2026-09-23-mokola-market-newsletter.md` first — that's Kofi's adopted real example, the primary voice anchor for this skill (edited by Kofi, not just AI-drafted brand voice). Then read `agents/marketing-team.md` (shared rules) and `docs/brand-voice.md`, every session. Check `docs/product-catalog-notes.md` before mentioning any specific SKU.

## Input

If not already given, ask what the email is about (a story, a launch, an episode) and what source material to draw from.

## What to produce

One email issue, written as if Kofi is writing personally:

- **Subject line**: 2 variants, for A/B testing. Short, may be punchy.
- **Preview text**
- **Body**: one story (a farm moment, a market memory, or one spice fact from the knowledge base with attribution) and one action. The body is not ad copy — keep the reflective register, not punchy phrasing.
- **CTA**

## Rules

- Every email needs an unsubscribe line and the company name, Fudi People Ltd. Assume only consented recipients.
- Do not state allergen information unless confirmed; link to the product page instead.
- Only mention dry blends and chilli oils that are actually published.
- No health claims. If Sanko Plum Wine is the subject, flag UK alcohol-advertising rules (18+, no targeting minors) and its `[OPEN]` licensing status rather than drafting sales copy.

## Save

Save to `email/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. End with a short "Needs Kofi" list.

## Learning from Kofi's edits

If Kofi edits `email/2026-09-23-mokola-market-newsletter.md` (or adopts a different issue as his real example later), re-read it before the next issue — his edits are the most current, most authoritative voice sample, ahead of `docs/brand-voice.md`.
