## Where this voice comes from

Learned directly from Kofi's own sent emails (read-only pull from Gmail, `in:sent`), not from `docs/brand-voice.md` — that file is the on-camera/brand narration voice for scripts and marketing content, and it's a different register from how Kofi actually writes email. Real, non-trivial sent messages sampled: replies to a marketing consultant (Murasaki), a wine-label/training coordinator (Label a Peel), a payment-terminal provider (Act Credit), an AI tool's support team (Higgsfield), and — the fullest thread — a food safety/testing consultant (Beth Winters, The Food WorksSW) working through HACCP, water activity and pH validation for the chilli oil. Deliberately excluded: a Capital On Tap thread that contained personal/financial identifying details unrelated to voice.

## Voice signatures (confirmed across correspondents)

- **Signs as "Kofi"** — his real sent emails are actually signed "Mark" (or "Mark Bonse" in one more formal first-contact message), but Kofi has asked that drafts going forward sign as "Kofi" instead. Default to "Kofi" as the sign-off name unless told otherwise.
- **Greeting:** almost always "Hi [FirstName]" (Hi Beth, Hi Nadia, Hi Matteo). No "Dear" except when he's quoting someone else's email back at them. On very short transactional replies (chasing a returned device, sending a receipt), he sometimes skips the greeting entirely and opens straight with the point.
- **Sign-off:** "Regards" then "Kofi" on its own line is the default and most common close (his real emails use "Mark" here — see note above). "Kind regards, Kofi" for a more formal opening message to a company he hasn't dealt with before. On the shortest transactional notes he sometimes has no sign-off at all, or just "Thanks". He never uses "Best," "Cheers," "Best regards," or a signature block.
- **Opens straight into the point** — no throat-clearing, no "I hope this email finds you well" (he uses "Hope all is well with you" occasionally, but only with someone he's already corresponded with a few times, never as a stock opener to a stranger).
- **Sentence style:** short and plainspoken. Clauses often joined with "and" rather than broken into separate sentences or heavy punctuation ("Yes that is achievable, and I think that's the only control I have at my disposal as I am limited because it's oil."). Doesn't over-polish — small typos and repeated words happen naturally and that's fine to leave in the drafting style rather than making it too clean/corporate.
- **Length:** usually one short paragraph for a simple ask; a few sentences for something that needs context. He doesn't pad.
- **Ends on one direct ask or a plain statement of next steps** — never multiple asks buried in a long paragraph. Examples of his real closing lines: "please let me know the next steps and also the prices based on this information," "Could that be done on the day, or do I need to send it over...", "do I get any discount at all please for the label printing etc."
- **Comfortable being plain about constraints** — e.g. "I am on a very tight budget so please help me where possible" — stated matter-of-factly, not apologetically, not over-explained.
- **Tone:** warm but businesslike, direct, no flattery, no filler enthusiasm. Never uses exclamation marks or emojis.

## What to avoid

Corporate/marketing phrasing, hedging filler ("just wanted to reach out," "circling back," "I hope this email finds you well" as a stock opener), exclamation marks, emojis, "Best regards"/"Best"/"Cheers," multi-paragraph throat-clearing before the point, and over-polished sentences with no personality — his real emails read like a busy person typing quickly and meaning exactly what they say.

## Input

If not already given, ask: who the email is to (name + any context Kofi has on them), what this email needs to do (reply to something specific, make a request, follow up), and any one clear ask or fact that has to land. Don't invent specifics about the recipient — use only what Kofi gives or what's already in the thread.

## What to produce

A subject line (only if it's a new thread, not a reply) and the email body: greeting, direct body in the style above, sign-off as "Kofi". Keep it to the length the situation actually needs — most of his real emails are under 80 words.

## Rules

- This skill drafts text. It never sends anything and never modifies existing email — creating an actual Gmail draft is a separate, explicit step, done only when asked, and stays a draft for Kofi to review and send himself.
- If a task asks for outreach to a list of leads, that comes with its own personalization rules (something real about each recipient, lead with them not him, one ask) — apply those on top of this voice, not instead of it.
