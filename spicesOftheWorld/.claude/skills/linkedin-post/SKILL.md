## Before writing

Read `social/2026-09-23-mokola-market-linkedin.md` first — that's Kofi's adopted real example, the primary voice anchor for this skill (edited by Kofi, not just AI-drafted brand voice). Then read `agents/marketing-team.md` (shared rules) and `docs/brand-voice.md`, every session. LinkedIn's audience skews toward wholesale buyers, press, and people interested in the founder story rather than end consumers, so pull context from `docs/business-plan.md` where relevant.

## Input

If not already given, ask what the post is about (an episode, a milestone, a product, a piece of the founder story) and what source material to draw from.

## What to produce

One LinkedIn post:

- **Hook line**: 1-2 sentences, the first thing visible before "see more" — concrete, not a slogan.
- **Body**: 3-6 short paragraphs, one concrete story or detail (a farm moment, a market memory, a milestone), written in the reflective first-person voice — not ad copy, no emoji stacking.
- **Soft CTA**: point to fudipeople.com, or, for a wholesale-facing post, an invitation to get in touch about stocking the range.
- **3-5 hashtags**.

## Rules

- No health claims. No allergen statements unless confirmed.
- Do not mention or promote wet-only or unpublished products as available.
- Do not put words in Kofi's mouth about personal history that isn't in the docs — use `[NEEDS KOFI: ...]`.
- If the post touches Sanko Plum Wine, flag alcohol-advertising rules and its `[OPEN]` licensing status rather than drafting sales copy.

## Save

Save to `social/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>-linkedin.md`. End with a short "Needs Kofi" list.
