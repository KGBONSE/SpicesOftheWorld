**File:** `2026-09-23-fudi-people-lead-list.csv` (same folder) — open in Excel/Numbers/Sheets.
**Built with:** the Clay connector (company + contact search, email enrichment) plus plain web
research where Clay came up empty. Not published or sent anywhere — for Kofi's review only.

## Ideal customer profile (confirmed with Kofi before building the list)

- **Business types:** independent grocers & delis, restaurants & food service, specialty/gourmet
  food retailers, corporate/gift & hospitality buyers, vegan shops.
- **Priority categories** (real supply history / chilli oil already served): African & Caribbean
  restaurants, and pizza/pasta/Italian restaurants.
- **Fit criteria:** London/UK, small/independent businesses, buyer/owner-level contact, mostly
  skipping the big chains but leaving room for a few larger/well-known names.
- **Disqualifiers:** large supermarket chains, no fresh/specialty food angle, outside the UK.

## What's in the file

**24 real, named companies, 27 contact rows** (some companies have 2–3 contacts). Every company
was independently verified (Clay's company database, or a real published restaurant guide/website)
— nothing here is guessed or AI-generated.

A **Confidence** column is on every row, because contact quality varied a lot by category:

- **High (14 contacts, 9 companies)** — a named person with a verified work email in Clay
  (pattern-matched to the company's own domain, e.g. `daniele@saleepepe.co.uk`). These are ready
  to email today: Chuku's, Enish, Sale e Pepe, Garum London, Villa di Geggiano, Bocconcino,
  White Rabbit, Rondanini UK (importer/wholesaler), Scandinavian Kitchen.
- **Medium (5 rows)** — a named person or a real general enquiries inbox, but no verified personal
  email: Akoko, Chishuru, Flavour Hub, Piazza Italiana.
- **Low (8 rows)** — a real, verified business with no reliable named contact yet: phone/website/
  social is the way in. Includes Aso Rock, Chaconia, Umana Yana, and a few small independent
  Italian spots (Burro e Salvia, Mele E Pere, Rossella, The River Cafe, Curd & Cure).

## Data-quality note (read before emailing)

Before running this at scale, I tested Clay's contact search on 3 Italian restaurants and the
results were rough: only 1 of 6 "contacts" got an email, and that one was tied to a LinkedIn
profile claiming to be a sole proprietor since 1962 — implausible for those businesses. Several
other contacts had locations that clearly didn't match London (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Egypt),
almost certainly cross-contamination from Scalini's and Gastronomica's international franchises.
I flagged this to Kofi and we agreed on a hybrid approach: keep whatever Clay found cleanly, and
fall back to public info (phone/website/social) everywhere else, rather than presenting shaky
data as solid.

Three rows in the CSV are flagged **Low** specifically because the Clay contact's location doesn't
match the London business (Il Baretto, Scalini, Gastronomica) — their real phone/website is listed
instead, and any named contact for them should be double-checked before using.
