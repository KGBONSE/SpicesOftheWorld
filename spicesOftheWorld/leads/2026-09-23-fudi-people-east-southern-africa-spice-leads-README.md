So this closes out the Africa arc as far as it can currently go — 3 of the 8 regions have a real

## What's in this file

**10 rows, 9 unique new companies** (St. Marcus Fine Foods repeats from the yaji list, since it
fits both), split by region:

- **East Africa / Pilau Masala (7):** Sabiib Somali Restaurant (Time Out's #1 East African
  restaurant in London), Mwendo Kasi (literally "Zanzibar Street Food"), Exceline, Jambo Zamsareh,
  Maandeeq (see caution below), plus two grocers — Owino Supermarket (Ugandan/Kenyan/Rwandan,
  trading since 1998) and East African Foods (online distributor).
- **Southern Africa / Durban Curry Masala (3):** Bunny UK (a Durban-curry meal-kit brand founded
  by someone from Durban itself — about as on-the-nose as a fit gets), The Savanna (14 London
  stores plus Wimbledon, South Africa's biggest UK retail presence), and St. Marcus (reused from
  the yaji list).

**One data-quality catch worth knowing about:** the obvious domain for "Maandeeq" restaurant
(`maandeeqs.com`) turned out to belong to a *different* restaurant of the same name in Hargeisa,
Somalia — not the real Woolwich, London one from Time Out's list. I didn't use that contact. Same
kind of mismatch as the Il Baretto/Scalini/Gastronomica flags on the first list — small, common
restaurant names collide easily, so I check before using rather than assume a name match is the
right business.
