# Fudi People — vegan & vegetarian restaurants and shops — 2026-09-23

**File:** `2026-09-23-fudi-people-vegan-vegetarian-leads.csv` (same folder).
**Built with:** the same hybrid method as the other four lists — web research against real
restaurant/shop guides and each business's own site (Clay wasn't run on this batch, since it's
consistently added little for small independents in every prior round).

## Why this list

You flagged that vegan and vegetarian restaurants and shops should be part of the ICP too. This
is a new category cutting across the whole product range rather than one spice blend — the chilli
oil and the dry spice blends are all vegan-friendly, so anywhere serving or stocking plant-based
food is a fit, independent of which region's blend they'd use.

## What's in this file

**12 companies, 12 rows**, split roughly in two:

- **Restaurants (8):** three standouts with real, dedicated inboxes — **Holy Carrot** (award-winning
  plant-based, 2 sites, also has an `events@` address for catering), **Plates** (the UK's first
  Michelin-starred vegan restaurant), and **Bubala** (high-profile vegetarian Middle Eastern group,
  3 London sites). Also **Oliveira Kitchen** (organic veg/vegan, Shoreditch), **Naifs** (Peckham,
  flagged below), **En Root** and **Andu Ethiopian Vegan Cafe** (both real, phone-only), and
  **Rasa** (flagged below).
- **Shops (4):** Earth Natural Foods (Kentish Town wholefoods), GreenBay Supermarket (London's
  first 100% vegan supermarket, since 2015), Organic For The People (Ealing), and Black Cat Cafe
  (Hackney vegan cafe). All four are real but thinner on contact info than the restaurants — three
  of the four have no confirmed email, just a site contact form or phone.

## Two data-quality flags — read before contacting

- **Naifs** (Peckham) has a real, dedicated email, but its own site currently says regular service
  is suspended, with only limited weekend hours running. Worth a call to confirm it's actually
  trading before pitching.
- **Rasa** (South Indian vegetarian, Stoke Newington/Mayfair) would be one of the strongest spice
  fits on this list if it's still open, but the sources disagreed: one directory listing said the
  Stoke Newington branch had closed or relocated, while Tripadvisor still shows current listings
  for both branches. I couldn't get through to the restaurant group's own site to settle it, so
  it's flagged Low with no contact info rather than presented as solid — needs a manual check.

**Also excluded, not used:** two shops that came up in the initial vegan-shop research turned out
to be closed — **Vx: Secret Society of Vegans** (King's Cross) and **Ms Cupcake** (Brixton bakery)
— both confirmed shut via Time Out and HappyCow respectively. Same discipline as the Kenza/Maandeeq
catches on the earlier lists: didn't want to hand you dead leads.

## Confidence key (same as the other four lists)

High = a real, dedicated inbox confirmed on the business's own site. Medium = a real contact with
something unconfirmed about it (here, Naifs' suspended-service notice). Low = real, verified
business, but only a phone number, directory listing, or no confirmed contact at all.
