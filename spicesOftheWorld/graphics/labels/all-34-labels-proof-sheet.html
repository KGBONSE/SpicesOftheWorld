How to read this sheet. This is a proof sheet for review, not individual export files — once a continent's palette and copy are approved, each blend gets cut as its own physical (95mm round) and digital (1200×1200px) file, same as Yaji's. Note: subheads and dish tie-ins are pulled directly from each episode script's own on-screen text, not invented. Products still tagged wet-only in the catalog (Yassa, Niter Kibbeh, Pepper Soup Spice, Harissa, Mbongo Mix, Zhug, Taklia, Vindaloo Paste, Yangnyeomjang, Chilli Black Bean Sauce, Chimichurri, Tucupí, Leche de Tigre) still get a label design here — packaging and sell-readiness are separate questions.
